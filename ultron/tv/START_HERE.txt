ULTRON - AI trade council for TradingView
==========================================

WHAT YOU NEED
  1. A free TradingView account (tradingview.com, or the TradingView Desktop app).
  2. ULTRON_indicator.pine  <- indicator 1, ULTRON Council: the AI that says BUY / WAIT with entry, stop, target, size
  3. ULTRON_Radar.pine      <- indicator 2, ULTRON Radar: forecasts big move / small move for the next 12 bars, plus
                               trend, stop, fee check and position size. Works on ANY chart and timeframe.
  4. ULTRON_strategy.pine   <- optional: shows how the Council's rules did in the past on any chart
  4. The free TradingView phone app, if you want buy/sell alerts on your phone.


INSTALL (2 minutes, one time)
  1. Open TradingView and open a chart. Use one of these:
       CRYPTO, any timeframe (best on 1h and 4h):
         COINBASE:BTCUSD  ETHUSD  SOLUSD  DOGEUSD  SHIBUSD  PEPEUSD  BONKUSD  WIFUSD  FLOKIUSD
       STOCKS / ETFs / FOREX / GOLD / OIL, DAILY (1D) chart only:
         SPY QQQ IWM DIA AAPL MSFT NVDA AMZN GOOGL META TSLA JPM AMD NFLX
         XIU RY TD ENB SHOP CNQ  EURUSD GBPUSD USDJPY USDCAD AUDUSD  GLD SLV USO
  2. At the bottom of the chart, click "Pine Editor".
  3. Delete everything in the editor.
  4. Open ULTRON_indicator.pine with Notepad, press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C, and paste it into the editor (Ctrl+V).
  5. Click "Save" (name it ULTRON), then click "Add to chart".
  6. Do steps 2-5 again with ULTRON_Radar.pine (click "Open" -> "New indicator" in the Pine Editor first).
     Put the Radar table in another corner: Radar gear icon -> Inputs -> Table position -> Top left.
  Done. Both tables appear on the chart.


HOW TO READ ULTRON RADAR
  "LOUD · big move likely"     -> the next 12 bars will probably move a lot (direction unknown). Good time to look
                                  for trades. Shows how often LOUD was right in testing.
  "QUIET · small move likely"  -> the next 12 bars will probably barely move. Fees eat small moves: stand aside.
  "Fee cost"                   -> OK / costly / too expensive for the stop shown. Skip "too expensive".
  "Long stop / target / Size"  -> where the stop and 2R target would be for a buy now, and how much to buy.

HOW TO READ ULTRON COUNCIL
  "WAIT · no setup now"        -> do nothing.
  "BUY · limit order waiting"  -> place a LIMIT BUY at the "Buy at" price, with the "Stop-loss" and the
                                  "Sell (target)" prices shown, for the "Size" shown.
  "IN TRADE"                   -> your order filled; leave the stop and target in place.
  "Flat: ..."                  -> this chart or timeframe had no reliable edge in testing, so ULTRON stays out.
  Blue background              -> LOUD: a big move is likely soon (direction unknown).
  On the chart: BUY label with blue (buy), red (stop) and green (target) lines; SELL label when it ends.


GET ALERTS ON YOUR PHONE (so you don't have to watch)
  1. Click the "Alert" button (clock icon) at the top of TradingView.
  2. Condition: ULTRON  ->  "Any alert() function call".
  3. Tick "Notify in app", click Create.
  4. Install the TradingView app on your phone and log in. ULTRON's BUY and SELL alerts arrive there.
  Make one alert per chart you want watched (for example BTC 1h, ETH 1h, SPY 1D).


SET IT UP FOR YOU (gear icon on the ULTRON indicator -> Inputs)
  Account size ($), risk per trade (%), and your exchange's fees. Everything else can stay as it is.
  "Simple view" off = the full table (backtest, trend, agents, regime).


OPTIONAL: SEE PAST RESULTS ON ANY CHART
  Paste ULTRON_strategy.pine into a new Pine Editor tab the same way, Add to chart, and open "Strategy Tester"
  at the bottom.


RULES
  - ULTRON never places orders. You place them on your exchange or broker.
  - Paper-trade first (TradingView has a free Paper Trading account under "Trading Panel").
  - Past results are not a forecast. Not financial advice.
