# ULTRON indicators: full backtest on every timeframe

Generated by `ultron/tools/report_all.py` from `ultron/tv/backtest/results.json`, which `ultron/tools/backtest_all.py`
produced from the same data and trained models the two indicators are built from. No number here was typed by hand.
Past results after costs, not a forecast, not financial advice.

## How to read the numbers

- **Periods.** *Fit* = data the models learned from (before 20 Mar 2025). *Validation* = 20 Mar – 22 Dec 2025, used only
  to accept or reject changes. *Test* = since 23 Dec 2025, never used to choose anything: the honest number.
- **Right X% (N calls).** Of the N bars where the Radar made that call, the share where it came true.
  LOUD comes true when the next 12 bars' high-to-low range lands in the top third of the last month's 12-bar
  ranges. QUIET comes true when it lands in the bottom third. **Chance** = how often that happens on any bar.
- **R.** One R = the amount you risk on a trade (entry to stop). +0.2R per trade = on average you made 20% of what
  you risked, after fees and slippage.
- **Win rate.** Share of trades that closed with a profit after fees. A high win rate alone means nothing: a
  strategy can win 90% of the time and still lose money if the losses are bigger than the wins. Read win rate
  together with avg R.
- **PF (profit factor).** Total won ÷ total lost. Above 1 = profitable.
- **Return / max DD.** On a $10,000 paper account with the Council's own position sizes. Max DD = worst drop from a
  high point.
