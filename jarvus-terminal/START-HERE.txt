JARVUS TERMINAL + ULTRON BRAIN  (Windows)

RUN IT
  1. Unzip this folder anywhere. Keep JarvusTerminal.exe in it.
  2. Double-click JarvusTerminal.exe. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": More info -> Run anyway (once).
  3. A black window opens and your browser goes to http://127.0.0.1:8787 (paste it in if it doesn't).
     Keep the black window open: closing it stops Jarvus and every bot.

WHAT'S NEW: THE ULTRON BRAIN
  The smartest part of the terminal is now the ULTRON councils: the trained AI that also powers the ULTRON TradingView
  indicators and ULTRON Watch. 46 ULTRON bots join the fleet and start by themselves with autopilot:
    ULTRON council 1h   BTC, ETH, SOL  +  DOGE, SHIB, PEPE, BONK, WIF, FLOKI
    ULTRON council 4h   the same 9 coins (runs on the 1h chart, acts when a 4-hour candle closes)
    ULTRON daily        SPY QQQ IWM DIA AAPL MSFT NVDA AMZN GOOGL META TSLA JPM AMD NFLX, XIU RY TD ENB SHOP CNQ,
                        EURUSD GBPUSD USDJPY USDCAD AUDUSD, GLD SLV USO
  Each bot buys only when its council's trained brain approves (25 agents, volatility gate, trend, learned edge,
  fee check), then manages the trade: stop-loss at the council's level, target at 2R, time limit.
  The Fleet Brain starts from ULTRON's measured walk-forward results for each market, priced at your fees.

PAPER MONEY (the default)
  "Your money" at the top of Command is the paper account, live: it moves with every trade and every price tick, and
  open trades are valued at the market price. Change the balance any time (Change balance / Fleet tab).

REAL MONEY (only when you switch it on yourself)
  Real money stays OFF until you do all of this, on the Live money page:
   1. Connections -> Connect an account:
        NDAX or Kraken for crypto, Alpaca for US stocks and ETFs (Toronto stocks, forex and the ETFs of other
        countries stay paper: none of these brokers trades them).
      Create the API key on the exchange's website with TRADING permission only - never withdrawal or deposit.
      Type the key into the app only: it is stored encrypted on your PC. Never send a key to anyone, including in chat.
      The connection test must pass.
   2. Live trading authorisation: tick the account, set the total amount the bots may use and a daily loss limit,
      and type  I UNDERSTAND THIS TRADES REAL MONEY.
   3. Command -> pick a bot -> LIVE mode -> readiness checks -> START LIVE.
  Which ULTRON bots may go live: the terminal only allows a bot whose strategy was positive on the untouched test at
  your fees, after at least 20 paper trades with a positive average on that bot. At NDAX fees that is currently
  ULTRON 1h (BTC/ETH/SOL), ULTRON 4h (meme coins) and ULTRON daily (US stocks/ETFs through Alpaca). The others keep
  paper trading until they earn it.
  The readiness check also asks for the strategy version to be approved: Research -> run a walk-forward on it ->
  Approve. ULTRON trades only a few times a year on any one market, so that one-market test can never reach the
  30 test trades it wants: approving it means typing  I ACCEPT THE FAILED CHECKS  (recorded). Its real evidence
  is the many-market walk-forward below and in "ULTRON backtest.md".

  HOW OFTEN ULTRON TRADES (be patient - it is picky on purpose)
  Counted on the last 12 months of real prices (Oct 2025 - Oct 2026), buy signals per bot:
    ULTRON 1h BTC/ETH/SOL   20-26 each        ULTRON 1h meme coins   14-26 each
    ULTRON 4h BTC/ETH/SOL   8-13 each         ULTRON 4h meme coins   0-3 each
    ULTRON daily            0-28 each (most stocks/ETFs 15-25; forex 2-3; META 0, NFLX 2)
  About 50 a month for all 46 bots together, a bit fewer actual trades (a bot already in a trade waits).
  These counts show how often it trades, not how much it makes. For results, see TESTED RESULTS below.
  So 20 paper trades on ONE bot takes about a year. The Live trading authorisation has a box to waive the paper
  record. Ticking it skips your own proof - only do it with an amount you can afford to lose.
  Back to paper at any time: "Revoke live authorisation" pauses every live bot; EMERGENCY STOP blocks all new orders.
  Your live account's money, trades and P&L show on the Live money page and in Your money.

  IMPORTANT: the NDAX and Kraken order connections were written to their published APIs but have never been run
  against the real exchanges (no keys were ever available to test them). Kraken's request signing matches the
  worked example in Kraken's own documentation; nothing more than that has been checked. Start with the smallest
  amounts, keep the daily loss limit low, and check the first orders on the exchange's own website.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

  Antivirus deleted the exe: some antivirus programs flag unsigned programs; restore it or add an exception.
