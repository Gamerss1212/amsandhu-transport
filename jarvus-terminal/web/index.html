Every bot
Each bot runs one strategy on one market with its own rules, stops and exits. The brain checks every entry before it happens. Click a bot to see exactly why it did what it did.
botstrategymarketstatepositionlast decision
1

Cost gate

Fees and spread as a share of the stop ("cost in R"). Above 0.33R the trade is refused; 0.20–0.33R trades at half size.

2

Volatility gate

A trained model forecasts whether the next hours will be LOUD or QUIET. QUIET: no new trades. LOUD: 0.6× size. It says how much, never which way.

3

Learned score

Expected R per trade from each strategy's tested history plus every closed paper trade, by market and regime. Negative edge: refused.

4

Bench & size

Bots that keep losing are benched until their shadow trades recover. Size scales with the edge and the evidence.

Step 1 · connect a broker
Your API key and secret are sent from this page to Jarvus on this computer and saved encrypted for your Windows user. They are never shown again, never logged and never leave this computer except to sign requests to that broker. Create keys WITHOUT withdrawal permission.
Step 2 · arm real-money trading
Which bots have earned real money
A bot qualifies only when it was positive on the untouched test data at its venue's real costs AND has at least 20 paper trades with a positive average. The list is re-checked before every live order, so bots join and leave by themselves.

The bots start with Jarvus and decide everything themselves: what to trade, how much, when to get out. If they ever stop unexpectedly they restart within a minute. Only Stop turns them off.
Safety switches
Pause: no new entries; open trades keep their stops and exits. Emergency stop: closes every paper position, blocks new trades and disarms real money until cleared.
