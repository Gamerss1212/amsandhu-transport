How to read this

Heat blends how fast a market already moves with whether it is speeding up or winding tight. High heat means a big move is likely. The direction is a coin flip.
Agree counts how many independent strategy families fired at once. Twelve trend strategies saying the same thing is one opinion, so they are grouped first.
Cost is the round trip as a share of your risk. Above 33% the trade is refused: fees would eat it before the idea gets a chance.
BUY means the structural boxes are ticked and the cost gate passed. It is not a forecast. Every plan carries a stop that defines the loss in advance.

The swarm

Every loaded strategy is run against every scanned market on each scan. Votes are grouped into families first, because a dozen strategies that all say "price is above a rising average" are one idea and not twelve, and agreement across different families is the only kind worth counting.

Research

Backtests every strategy against every market over deep history, splits the period in two, and judges each strategy only on the half it was not built on. The weights the swarm uses come from here. Ranking is by excess over a control that buys with no analysis at all, because in a rising market the control makes money too, and anything that cannot beat it is measuring the weather.
