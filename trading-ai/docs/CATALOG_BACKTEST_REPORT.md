## Caveats that apply to every number above

- **Survivorship bias.** Stock universes are today's large companies; there is no point-in-time membership or delisting data. Those templates are capped at RESEARCH FURTHER.
- **Continuous futures.** Futures use the provider's continuous series, not real contract rolls; roll costs and roll timing are approximations. Futures portfolios are research-only (not deployable here).
- **Contract size.** Single-market tests start with 100,000 USD and size trend trades to a 15% volatility target. One ES or gold contract is worth several times that, so those signals cannot be traded at all; such runs carry CONTRACT_TOO_LARGE and say nothing about the signal. The micro E-mini (MES, one tenth of ES) is tested alongside for that reason.
- **Long-only adaptations.** Spot crypto and stock templates that are long/short in the literature are run long/flat here; that is a different strategy from the published one.
- **Short, recent histories.** Crypto has at most about eleven years of daily data (SOL far less), and intraday data from free providers covers weeks to months, so intraday tests have few trades.
- **Modelled costs.** Fees, spread and slippage are assumptions (edit them to match your broker); every template is also shown at 2× costs.
- **Multiple testing.** Every run is a trial; the Deflated Sharpe bar rises with every run in a family, including the runs in this batch.
